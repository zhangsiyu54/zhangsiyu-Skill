# Cohort discovery and screening protocol

## Search ledger

For every query record repository, exact search string, date, returned accessions, screening decision, exclusion reason, related publication, and suspected cohort overlap.

## Duplication controls

Compare accession links, author/institution, recruitment dates, sample identifiers, clinical summaries, and publication methods. Treat reprocessed copies of the same biological samples as one cohort.

## Compatibility checks

Do not pool effects until phenotype, tissue, comparator, direction, and statistical unit are aligned. Analyze distinct tissues, disease stages, treatment contexts, and assay types separately, then triangulate conclusions.

## Bias and sensitivity

Report small-study effects cautiously when the number of cohorts permits. Perform leave-one-study-out, alternative eligibility definitions, model choice sensitivity, and influence diagnostics. Preserve excluded and null cohorts in the ledger.
