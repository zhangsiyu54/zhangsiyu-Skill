Briefings in Bioinformatics. 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbae069 (large-scale benchmark of pathway/enrichment analysis method categories across curated disease expression datasets).
- Toro-Domínguez D, Wang C, Ellson-Lancho I, Martorell-Marugán J, López-Domínguez R, Carmona-Sáez P, Alarcón-Riquelme ME, Baribaud F. Briefings in Bioinformatics. 2025. https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbaf684 (compares GSVA, ssGSEA, singscore, z-score, PLAGE, and other single-sample scorers for patient stratification and clinical association use cases; recent publication, verify indexing/volume details before citing in a formal manuscript).
